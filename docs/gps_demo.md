# Three-minute portfolio demonstration

**0:00–0:45 · Research.** Open Market & Industry Intelligence. Select USD/CNY and move the cutoff before 28 July 2025 to hide the most recent sampled quote. Point to the publication date and source fields. Move down to the synthetic scenario and widen the supplier FX spread. Explain how the new cash projection uses synthetic scheduled flows and does not change historical postings.

**0:45–1:45 · Client solution.** Open Client Solutions at the default ledger date. Start with Harbor Components and trace the contingent liquidity recommendation to the five-day receipt-delay funding gap. Then switch to Jade Disbursements to show the domestic payment mix and exception focus. Download the editable proposal for either client and match the figures on slide 2 to the on-screen KPIs.

**1:45–2:40 · Commercial opportunity.** Open Commercial Opportunity. Review the cost components and identify which assumptions explain client savings. Set adoption to zero to show how bank revenue disappears while the upfront rollout cost remains. Restore the default and review the conditions for a meaningful payback.

**2:40–3:00 · Checks and limitations.** Show the automated test commands and the committed brief, proposal and business case. Describe the fictional clients, illustrative prices and what real invoice and servicing data would be required for a decision.
