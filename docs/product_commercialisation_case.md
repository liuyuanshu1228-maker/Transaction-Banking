# Product business case | Marketplace collections

**Decision.** Approve a limited pilot for fictional marketplace Pearl Commerce. Lead with virtual account collections. Scale only if the pilot confirms match quality, redeployable effort and sufficient adoption to cover fixed costs.

**Client problem.** The scenario assumes 12,000 monthly incoming receipts, of which 32% require manual attribution. Virtual identifiers would connect receipts to payer records while preserving the master corporate account. The rule selects collections when monthly receipts reach 1,000, at least 20% require manual attribution, and both client net value and per-client provider contribution are positive.

**Client value.** A 70% reduction in manual cases at six minutes per case and USD 30 per hour implies **USD 96,768** of annual effort value. At USD 450 per month plus USD 0.06 per receipt, the modeled annual fee is **USD 14,040**, leaving **USD 82,728** of net client value. The estimate assumes released staff time can be redeployed.

**Provider economics.** Of 40 fictional prospects, 35% adoption corresponds to 14 expected clients. Year-one costs include USD 180 monthly support, USD 0.02 per receipt and USD 600 onboarding per adopter, plus USD 30,000 fixed platform cost. Break-even requires four full-year equivalent clients at base volumes before excluded costs.

**Pilot gates.** Measure payer attribution, unmatched receipt ageing, duplicate handling, manual minutes and servicing cost. Confirm reference coverage, operational ownership and implementation effort before expansion. Commercial inputs are fictional. Model scope excludes acquisition expense, migration costs beyond onboarding, taxes, adoption ramp and ongoing attrition.
