# Market Intelligence Brief

## Published evidence

- The midpoint of the US policy target declined from 4.875% on 18 September 2024 to 4.375% on 18 December and stayed at 4.375% at the 18 June 2025 meeting. Treasury should check floating-rate debt repricing and deposit yield separately. [September decision](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20240918a.htm) · [December decision](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20241218a.htm) · [June decision](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20250618a.htm)
- USD/CNY fell from 7.2440 to 7.1679 and USD/SGD fell from 1.3448 to 1.2813 between the selected 24 January and 25 July 2025 observations. A fixed USD payable required fewer local units at the latter quotes, before execution spreads. [January H.10](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h10/20250127/) · [July H.10](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h10/20250728/)
- USD/HKD rose from 7.7878 to 7.8498 over those same samples. The direction differs from CNY and SGD, which matters when forecasting funding account by account. [January H.10](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h10/20250127/) · [July H.10](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h10/20250728/)
- The FSB's wholesale one-business-day credit measure rose from 93.2% in the 2025 report to 93.9% in 2026. Regional and segment differences still matter. Test beneficiary credit timing and data quality for each eligible corridor before setting operating expectations. [2025 FSB report](https://www.fsb.org/uploads/P091025-1.pdf) · [2026 FSB report](https://www.fsb.org/2026/11/g20-roadmap-for-enhancing-cross-border-payments-consolidated-progress-report-for-2026/)

## Application to the model

The snapshot uses selected historical observations and report years. Each record includes observation period, publication date, frequency, unit and source. The cutoff applies to publication dates. Historical values provide research context. The 30-day sensitivity engine uses independent synthetic treasury flows and account FX. Shocks reprice future flows and USD translation while leaving historical cash postings intact. The rate shock models incremental simple interest under assumed USD cash and debt balances. Results illustrate exposure under a specified scenario.
