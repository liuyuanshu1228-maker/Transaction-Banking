## Research framework

- **Cross-border payments.** Compare corridor and beneficiary credit time; check exception handling. [Reference](https://www.fsb.org/2026/11/g20-roadmap-for-enhancing-cross-border-payments-consolidated-progress-report-for-2026/)
- **Corporate liquidity.** Compare local cash with local buffers, then translate to USD for reporting. [Reference](https://careers.bankofamerica.com/en-us/company/organization/global-payments-solutions)
- **Working capital.** Compare due dates and actual cash timing; test the impact of collection delays. [Reference](https://www.wto.org/english/res_e/booksp_e/gvcreport2025-06_e.pdf)
- **FX exposure.** Separate USD payables from USD translation of existing operating cash. [Reference](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h10/20250728/)
- **Trade and supply-chain finance.** Ask for invoice terms, acceptance, tenor and counterparty evidence before sizing a facility. [Reference](https://www.wto.org/english/res_e/booksp_e/gvcreport2025-06_e.pdf)
